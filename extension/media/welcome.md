## What is HackLM Memory?

Copilot forgets everything between sessions. HackLM Memory fixes that.

As you chat, Copilot learns your project's conventions, decisions, and preferences — and writes them to `.memory/` markdown files in your workspace.

On every new session, those memories are automatically injected into `.github/copilot-instructions.md` so Copilot picks up where it left off.

**Nothing to configure.** Open a chat, and the memory layer is already active.
