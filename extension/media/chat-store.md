## Storing your first memory

Open Copilot Chat and ask something about your project — architecture, a naming convention, or a rule you follow. When Copilot learns something worth keeping, it calls `storeMemory` automatically.

You can also be explicit:

> "Remember: we use `zod` for all runtime validation."

Copilot will confirm before saving. Once stored, the memory appears in the **Memories** view immediately and is injected into every future session.
