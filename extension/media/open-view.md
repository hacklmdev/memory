## The Memories view

Open the Explorer sidebar and look for the **Memories** section (collapsed by default).

It shows all stored memories grouped by category:

| Category | Stores |
|----------|--------|
| **Instruction** | How Copilot should behave |
| **Quirk** | Project-specific surprises |
| **Preference** | Style, naming, and design choices |
| **Decision** | Architectural commitments |
| **Security** | Rules that must never be broken |

Click any entry to open its source file and jump to that line.
